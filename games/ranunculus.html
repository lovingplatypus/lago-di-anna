RANUNCULUS

A ranunculus hides dozens of petals in patient, concentric rings. Someone has turned them out of true.

Every ring carries exactly one dotted petal. The bloom is solved only when all the dots point straight up, twelve o'clock, and the color flows unbroken from the heart.

Drag any ring to turn it — but take care: in later blooms, rings turn their neighbors with them.
